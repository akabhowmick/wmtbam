Game setup

Add your players and questions, then start the show.

Players

Players take the hot seat in this order. Blank names become “Player 1”, “Player 2”, and so on.

Questions

How to format questions

Put the question on the first line and its four answers underneath, with a blank line between questions. The first answer is the correct one, unless you mark a different one with a *.

What is the capital of Australia?
Canberra
Sydney
Melbourne
Perth

One line per question also works, with the parts separated by | like this:

Which planet is the Red Planet? | Mars | Venus | Jupiter | Mercury

You can also paste rows straight from Google Sheets or Excel, or upload a .csv, with the columns in this order: Question, Correct, Wrong, Wrong, Wrong. Answers get shuffled during the game, so the order you list them in doesn’t matter.
